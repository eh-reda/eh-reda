## Who Am I?

I operate at the intersection of **Offensive Security, Enterprise Networking, and Systems Engineering**. Currently pursuing my **B.Tech in Cybersecurity at Elsewedy University of Technology**, I spend my time analyzing network topologies, hunting vulnerabilities, and building security tooling that solves real operational problems. Whether it's configuring Next-Gen Firewalls, inspecting raw packet captures, or evaluating Post-Quantum Cryptography readiness, I don't trust black boxes—I open them up to see how they tick.

* **Field Training & Industry Exposure:**
  * **Orange Egypt** *(Technology Dept)* — Penetration testing, vulnerability remediation, and enterprise network optimization.
  * **BARQ Systems** — Enterprise network security architecture, FortiGate NGFW policies, IPsec VPNs, and High Availability clusters.
  * **MCS (Masterpiece for Cybersecurity)** — Advanced offensive/defensive security operations, threat analysis, and infrastructure hardening.
* **Core Philosophy:** *A secure system isn't built by hiding the architecture; it's built by understanding every packet that crosses the wire.*
* **Off the Clock:** Competing in cybersecurity CTFs, automating Linux workflows via Bash, and Reverse Engineering protocols.
* *Your firewall says `DROP`, my packet crafted header says `ACCEPT`.*
